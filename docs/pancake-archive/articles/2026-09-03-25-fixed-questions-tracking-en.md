## What does generative AI search visibility tracking actually mean?

It means checking, on a fixed schedule, whether your brand gets mentioned or cited when someone asks an AI answer engine a question related to your business — and recording that in a way you can compare month to month.

That sounds simple. In practice, most companies skip it entirely because there's no equivalent of a rank tracker for ChatGPT the way there is for Google. You can't just look up your "position." There is no SERP. What you get instead is a generated answer that may or may not mention you, may or may not link to you, and may phrase things differently every time you ask.

So the tracking problem isn't technical — it's methodological. If you ask a different question every month, or phrase the same question slightly differently, you can't tell whether a change in the answer reflects a real shift in your visibility or just noise from the model. The fix is to stop improvising the questions and freeze them.

## Why we freeze 25 questions instead of asking whatever comes to mind

We freeze the question set at onboarding because a moving target can't be measured. If question 14 changes wording in March, you can't compare March to February — you're comparing two different experiments.

Here's how the 25 questions get built, in scoping, with the client — not for them:

- We start from the client's actual sales conversations: what do prospects ask before they buy, in their own words?
- We add category questions a prospect would type into ChatGPT or Perplexity before knowing the client's brand exists — "best [category] in [region]" style prompts.
- We add a handful of comparison questions, where competitors are named directly.
- We lock the wording. No rephrasing, no "improving" a question mid-quarter, even if the answer it returns is disappointing.

Once frozen, the same 25 questions get run every month, in the same order, in both English and French where the client operates bilingually. That repetition is the entire point — it's what turns a one-off AI visibility snapshot into a visibility *trend*.

## What we run and what we record

Each month, the 25 questions get submitted to ChatGPT and Perplexity. For each answer, we record three things:

1. Whether the brand is mentioned at all — by name, even without a link.
2. Whether the brand is cited with a link, and to which specific page.
3. Which other domains got mentioned or cited on that same question — this is the competitive map.

That third point matters more than it sounds. A client who isn't mentioned yet can still learn a lot from seeing which of their competitor's pages the AI engine is pulling from. If a competitor's pricing page or comparison article is getting cited repeatedly, that tells you what kind of content the model considers a trustworthy answer — and what's currently missing on your own site.

We don't score this into a single index number. A composite "AI visibility score" hides more than it reveals — it can't tell you whether you dropped because your brand fell out of favor or because the model changed how it phrases answers to that specific question. We'd rather hand over the raw table: 25 rows, mention yes/no, citation yes/no, competing domains, month over month. It's less elegant on a dashboard. It's also harder to fake.

## How this fits into the monthly report

The AI-engine tracking isn't a separate deliverable — it's one of the three blocks in the monthly document every retainer client receives, alongside the work done in the codebase that month and the competitive standing on Google. If a page we optimized last month starts getting cited on Perplexity this month, that's the kind of change the report is built to surface, because the same 25 questions were asked both times.

This is also why the method matters more than the tool. Several platforms — SE Ranking's [AI visibility tracker](https://seranking.com/ai-visibility-tracker.html) and Keyword.com's [AI brand monitoring](https://keyword.com/ai-search-visibility/) among them — offer dashboards that check brand mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI Overviews. They're built for scale, checking many keywords across many domains. What we do is narrower and slower by design: a fixed panel of questions specific to one client's actual buyers, re-run identically, with a human reading every answer rather than only the aggregate count. For an SME with one brand to track and a limited number of real buying questions, that trade-off tends to favor precision over breadth. If you want a fuller comparison of what these tools do and don't cover for a company your size, we've broken that down separately in [AI Search Visibility Tools Compared](/ai-search-visibility-tools-compared-what-smes-actually-need-to-track).

## Why AI answer engines don't behave like Google

AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity are pulling from an index, but they're not returning a ranked list — they're generating a synthesized answer, and the sources behind that answer shift depending on phrasing, session, and model updates. Search Engine Journal reported that 37% of users are now starting their searches in AI systems rather than traditional search engines, according to [a 2026 study covered by Search Engine Land](https://wpengine.com/blog/ai-visibility-tracking-tools/). That's a real shift in where the first impression happens, and it's why treating AI visibility as a nice-to-have next to Google rankings is increasingly a gap in the marketing plan, not a footnote.

The practical consequence for an in-house marketing manager: you can no longer treat "we rank on Google" as the whole picture. A prospect who never sees your Google listing because they asked ChatGPT instead is still a lost opportunity — you just have no visibility into it unless you're checking. We've written more on the underlying causes in [Why Your Brand Is Invisible on ChatGPT and Perplexity](/why-your-brand-is-invisible-on-chatgpt-and-perplexity-and-how-to-fix-it), and on what a serious audit of that presence should actually cover in [AI Visibility Audit: What It Should Actually Measure](/ai-visibility-audit-what-it-should-actually-measure-and-what-most-skip).

## What this method doesn't do

It doesn't track every question a prospect might ever type — 25 is a deliberate limit, chosen so the panel stays reviewable by a person every month rather than becoming another dashboard nobody reads. It doesn't cover every AI engine either: we track ChatGPT and Perplexity because that's where the client's prospects are shown to be asking, not Gemini or Copilot by default. And it doesn't produce a single visibility score you can drop into a slide without context — the honest output is a table, with gaps, that you can go back and check against the actual answers we pulled that month.

## FAQ

### How do you monitor AI search visibility without a rank tracker?

By replacing the idea of a rank with a fixed, repeatable question set. We freeze 25 questions at onboarding and run them identically every month across ChatGPT and Perplexity, recording brand mentions, citations, and which competitor pages show up instead. Consistency in the questions is what makes month-to-month comparison possible.

### Is there a free AI visibility tracker available?

Some platforms, including SE Ranking, offer a limited number of free daily checks against your domain and competitors before requiring a trial signup. These are useful for a quick spot-check but don't give you the repeated, identical-question tracking needed to see a trend over months.

### Is AI search optimisation just buzzword marketing, or is it measurable?

It's measurable when it's operationalized with a concrete method. Our approach freezes 25 questions at onboarding, runs them monthly across ChatGPT and Perplexity, and maps exactly which external pages get cited for each one. The results are tracked over time — not asserted in a slide deck.

### What's different about your approach compared to an AI visibility tool?

Tools like SE Ranking or Keyword.com scale across many keywords and many domains with a dashboard. We run a narrow, fixed panel of questions specific to one client's real buyers, and a person reads every generated answer rather than relying only on an aggregate score. For a single SME brand, that trade-off favors precision over breadth.

### We already work with an SEO agency — what does this add?

Most agencies audit and advise on AI visibility without implementing anything in your codebase or running a repeatable measurement. We do both: our developers make the fixes directly, and the same 25-question panel gets tracked monthly so you can see whether those fixes actually changed how ChatGPT and Perplexity answer questions about your brand.
